Campus Resource Wastage Reporting System

Problem Statement

Leaking taps and pipelines around the campus often go unnoticed for a long time, leading to a significant amount of water being wasted before anyone reports or fixes it. Similarly, lights and fans in classrooms and labs are frequently left switched on even when the rooms are empty, leading to unnecessary electricity consumption.

Solution: A simple portal where students can log in and report wastage instantly.
